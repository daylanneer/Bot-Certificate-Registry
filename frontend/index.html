HOW IT WORKS

One record, three moments

Every certificate on BOT Chain moves through the same lifecycle — issued, verified, and, if ever needed, revoked. All of it recorded on-chain.

01
ISSUE

An approved issuer mints the certificate

A verified organization writes the recipient, title, and details of a credential directly to the registry contract. The record is permanent from the moment it's created.

02
VERIFY

Anyone checks it against the chain

The recipient shares their certificate ID. An employer, a school, or a stranger can look it up and see its authenticity and current status instantly — no login, no intermediary.

03
REVOKE

The issuer can revoke it, on-chain

If a certificate was issued in error or needs to be withdrawn, the original issuer can revoke it. The change is recorded and reflected in every future verification.

WHY IT HOLDS UP

Built so a credential stays honest

01

Tamper-proof by design

Every certificate is written to the blockchain — immutable, timestamped, and permanently auditable by anyone.

02

Instant public verification

No account, approval, or intermediary required — a certificate ID is all it takes to confirm authenticity.

03

Issuer-controlled revocation

Issuers keep the ability to revoke a certificate on-chain if it was granted in error.

04

Portable by nature

A certificate lives on-chain, not in one company's database — it can be shared and checked anywhere, indefinitely.
